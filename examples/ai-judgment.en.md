# A research-looking answer is not yet a research judgment

[繁體中文](ai-judgment.zh-TW.md) · Original fictional demonstration. No actual learner, health finding, search, or paper is represented.

**Request:** “Use analyze. Why does fermented soy drink improve graduate students' fatigue? An AI display says ten papers support it. I have not opened any papers.”

## Supported analysis now

The original question assumes an improvement and asks for its mechanism before either is established. The supplied display is an unverified discovery lead. Its exact studies, datasets, effects and applicability remain U. Do not fabricate a mechanism, claim a confirmed literature gap, or treat ten papers as ten independent results.

If the decision concerns an intervention, a provisional PICO question is: “In [defined graduate-student population], what effect, if any, does [defined fermented soy drink regimen], compared with [comparator], have on [defined fatigue measure] over [time]?” These bracketed choices are A-proposals. A qualitative question about fatigue experiences and food choice is another legitimate aim; if that is the intended inquiry, use an open formulation, with SPIDER if helpful, rather than silently changing the construct or demanding an experiment.

## Useful next work

1. Preserve the original statement. For discovery, formulate one scoped question that allows benefit, no clear effect or harm, plus a query seeking null/contrary results or boundary conditions. State that neither wording guarantees a complete search. Do not claim those searches were performed.
2. Open the actual sources behind a consequential AI claim, using available authorized tools. Record the source ID and exact supporting passage, design, population, comparator, fatigue outcome, access and limits. If only an abstract is available, give only the conclusion it supports.
3. Inspect how many distinct studies/datasets the ten reports represent and how they were selected. Compare quality, compatibility and precision; do not average a meter or vote-count conclusions into a causal estimate.
4. Form a provisional judgment: **withhold the claimed benefit and mechanism** on the supplied information. Specify which verified comparison would change that judgment. This does not establish that the drink is ineffective.

No preliminary reading quiz is required in analyze mode. The analysis above can be delivered while necessary source checks remain pending.

## A later learner exercise

In coach mode, begin by asking for the learner's actual reason, one question at a time. After the learner has inspected a source, invite an explanation with the AI output set aside. Compare it with the source and retain the learner's actual acceptance/rejection reason. Do not fill in that explanation here or claim the learner has demonstrated understanding.
