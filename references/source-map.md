## Course-to-package design decisions

- The five-habit loop is preserved, with next evidence made an explicit feedback step.
- The later five-branch map is canonical. The four-branch exercise remains an introductory view; its [H]/[?] notation is explicitly mapped to A/U in [evidence-and-maps.md](evidence-and-maps.md).
- Learners retain their first reasons and own revision decisions. No private student examples are reused; the nutrition dialogue is newly fictionalized.
- The four-game synthesis becomes optional prompts. The incomplete Prisoner's Dilemma activity is not expanded into a claimed original classroom experiment.
- Source access, checking scope, publication status and scientific support are separate fields. E is not a validity stamp.
- Course search tips are retained as a need to form concepts/synonyms and inspect source context; search-engine-specific wildcard/Boolean syntax is not universalized.
- The package excludes unverified scientific-history, award and generic AI-future claims that are not needed for the reasoning method.

Version 0.2.0 adds teacher-facing lesson design, a time-balanced 120-minute workshop and 50-minute variant, six operational activity cards and blank classroom worksheets. The Prisoner's Dilemma payoff matrix and repeated-round protocol are new package designs inspired by the course concept, not recovered slide content. Activity timing and formative-assessment suggestions are design proposals, not measured learning outcomes.
