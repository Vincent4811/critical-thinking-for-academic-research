# Workflow: from first judgment to next evidence

This is the operating reference for the five habits. Output labels and map semantics are defined in [evidence-and-maps.md](evidence-and-maps.md); adapted tools are optional and defined in [thinking-tools.md](thinking-tools.md).

## Intake without an interview

Use the question, claim, paper excerpt, proposal, or observed result already supplied. Identify the requested decision and the evidence actually available. Preserve the initial wording and the user's stated reason; when no reason is supplied, mark it unprovided rather than reconstructing it as their belief.

For `analyze`, proceed with a provisional analysis if missing information does not prevent useful work. Ask only for a necessary fact that cannot be inferred or inspected. For `coach`, the learner's reasoning is itself the input: ask for their question or reason if absent, then wait. This pedagogical pause is not a permission request.

## Challenge

Separate the observation, explanation, and desired conclusion. Find the premise whose failure would most change the conclusion. Check whether the question starts by assuming the effect, mechanism, population, or preferred answer it should investigate.

**Done:** the starting position and one consequential premise are explicit, or the existing premise is already well supported. Do not manufacture a flaw to appear critical.

## Define

Turn a broad topic into an answerable uncertainty. Identify the relevant population or model, factor or phenomenon, comparison if appropriate, observable outcome, and time/context where it changes the question. Identify the experimental or observational unit when the design makes it consequential. For interviews, exploratory work, or theory development, use a question about experience, process, meaning, or conceptual relations rather than forcing an intervention/comparator format.

Distinguish the **question** from the **method**: a named assay, interview, model, or MindMap is a means of answering a question. A proposed operationalization is A until supported or chosen; do not silently replace the user's construct with an easier proxy.

**Done:** an answer could differ from the favored conclusion, and the target construct can be distinguished from its measurement. Missing design choices are labeled rather than fabricated.

## Evidence

For each material claim, inspect the best accessible source and retain an exact locator. Record what the source measured/reported and how far it answers the question. Check only consequential features: allocation, controls, confounding, unit of analysis, denominator/exclusions, missingness, units, statistic labels, uncertainty, and applicability.

A narrative summary is not participant-level data. Metadata verifies existence/status, not every result. A paper reporting a mechanism does not make every link in the mechanism measured. When sources conflict, preserve the conflicting statements, check version/context/status, and state the remaining uncertainty rather than averaging prose.

The evidence ledger should make clear whether an entry supports, limits, challenges, or does not answer the claim. Verify current correction/retraction status when material to using the source. If browsing is unavailable, mark status as not checked in this run.

**Done:** the supported part and unsupported extension of each consequential claim are visible, including access limitations. No source found is a search outcome, not proof that a research gap exists.

## Alternatives

Use credible explanations that fit the observation, including a measurement or selection explanation when relevant. For each, name an expected observation and the comparison or evidence that could distinguish it from the preferred explanation. A model or pathway diagram is a hypothesis, not a causal identification strategy.

Do not require two alternatives when only one serious explanation survives evidence. Explain the check that removed the alternatives. If several remain observationally equivalent, say what would separate them or why no feasible discriminator is available. An absence of a significant difference is not evidence of equivalence without the relevant precision and design.

Prefer a discriminating check over accumulating more evidence that would fit every hypothesis. "Cheapest" means proportionate and feasible under scientific, ethical, and practical constraints; it never authorizes an experiment or unsafe intervention.

**Done:** remaining explanations have discriminators, or their non-identifiability is explicit.

## Revise

Choose a supported action: **keep, narrow, change, or withhold**. State which evidence or limitation produced it. Preserve the original question so the change is traceable; a well-supported reaffirmation is a valid endpoint.

Choose the next evidence by: how much it could separate important explanations, feasibility, ethical acceptability, and whether the answer would change a decision. Specify where to look or what comparison/measurement is needed, expected distinguishing outcomes, and the update each would support. A proposed study is not a completed result. If no feasible test is available, deliver a bounded judgment and the reason; do not promise certainty.

**Done:** current judgment, reason, residual uncertainty, and a consequential next step are stated, or further checking cannot change the present decision.

## Coaching turns and ownership

Start with the learner's actual question and reason. Ask one load-bearing question about definition, evidence, alternatives, or revision. Give thinking time; in an asynchronous chat, stop after the question. Respond to what the learner said before choosing the next question. Offer a small scaffold if needed, not the completed revised question before they have attempted an answer.

At the end, the learner chooses Accepted / Partly accepted / Not accepted / Need verification for feedback and supplies a reason. Keeping the original question is legitimate when supported. If they ask for an example, label your worked example as a teacher/AI demonstration; never enter it in the record as the learner's answer.

For teacher preparation, a planned question sequence may be written in advance, but actual student responses and revision decisions remain empty until supplied. Do not expose an entire scripted interrogation to a learner by default.

## Hand off without expanding the assignment

An analysis may identify a literature search, power calculation, qualitative protocol, statistical analysis, or manuscript revision as the next step. Explain the need and inputs. Execute it only within the user's authorized request and the host's capabilities; identifying a need does not automatically start a full research or manuscript pipeline.

End when the current requested reasoning result is useful and bounded. Independent missing sources can remain pending. Do not delay a complete supported answer merely to fill every template field.
