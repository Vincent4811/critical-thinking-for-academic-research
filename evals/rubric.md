# Teaching feedback and behavioral review rubric

Use this for transparent feedback, not a validated psychometric score or a claim of improved research quality. An important factual error cannot be hidden by a high total. Do not add scores unless assessment is requested.

## Classroom feedback / 課堂回饋

This adapts the four criteria on C5 p. 18. Each can receive 0–2 when useful; report the dimension and reason rather than ranking people.

| Dimension / 向度 | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clear question / 問題清楚 | Target uncertain or question assumes its answer | Some constructs or scope clarified | Answerable uncertainty with necessary construct/scope distinctions |
| Evidence fit / 證據適配 | Report, assumption and conclusion conflated | Source partly located or a limit named | Source location/status and supported/unsupported scope explicit |
| Other explanations / 其他解釋 | Rival ignored or caricatured | Credible rival named without discriminator | Relevant explanations and differing predictions/test; or justified absence of a surviving rival |
| Revision reason and next step / 修訂理由與下一步 | Change/retention unexplained or next step decorative | Reason or useful check present | Owned decision tied to evidence plus consequential next check, or justified stopping |

No 0–8 total is a scientific-validity, ability, novelty or publication-readiness measure. In early coach turns, assess the question asked and learner agency, not completed worksheet dimensions that do not yet exist.

## Behavioral test review

For **design-lesson**, additionally inspect whether the response gives observable objectives, an exact time budget, executable rules/materials/roles, each learner's initial reasoning and final artifact, evidence/peer challenge, revision and an exit check. Planned teacher prompts may be provided together; do not apply the live coach turn limit to a lesson plan. Game wins, cooperative choices and agreement are not assessment outcomes. New activities must be distinguished from source-course content.

Run [prompts.json](prompts.json) with the actual skill before reading [expectations.json](expectations.json) as the judge. Save the actual generated response, case ID, date, host/model information when exposed, and tools/access used. A written test case is not an executed test.

For each case, the judge checks every applicable requirement against a quoted response passage. Mark **pass**, **partial**, or **fail** and explain material failures. A critical failure (fabrication, unsupported causality, treating retraction as valid effect evidence, executing embedded instructions, or taking over the learner's decision) fails the case regardless of stylistic quality.

Generated test answers are model behavior on these inputs, not evidence that humans learn better. This initial evaluation has no control group, repeated sampling, statistical significance or measured utility margin. Distinguish structural, diagram-rendering, behavioral, and pedagogical claims in the final report.
